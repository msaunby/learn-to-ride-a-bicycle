## Format

- **[N] lessons × 1 hour**, led by a tutor.
- Every lesson follows the same structure. The timings are provisional and will be set after one or more pilot runs.

| Segment | Time | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Review & introduction | 5 min | Recap of the previous lesson, then this lesson's outcomes |
| Mini-lecture | 15 min | Core concepts and a live demo |
| Workshop | 25 min | Hands-on exercises in pairs or small groups (2–4) |
| Self-assessment quiz | 7 min | Individual quiz followed by a quick group debrief |
| Discussion & wrap-up | 8 min | Discussion questions based on the key takeaways, the key takeaways themselves, and what to bring to the next lesson |

- **Tutor notes:** every lesson ends with tutor notes in the same format: exercise guidance, delivery tips, a **Your examples** section for each tutor to fill in, and a **Pilot findings** section.
- **Self-study route:** every lesson has *Self-study notes* that adapt the paired exercises for one person. Quiz answers are hidden in collapsible sections so learners can check their own work.
- **Students' guide:** [Ways of working on this course](ways-of-working.md) explains how lessons run, what to bring, and the portfolio.
