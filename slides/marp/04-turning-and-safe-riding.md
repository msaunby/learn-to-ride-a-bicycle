# Today: put the skills together

- **Slow before a turn**
  Choose a pace that feels controlled.
- **Look through the turn**
  Turn your head and eyes towards the open path.
- **Steer smoothly**
  Use a gentle curve rather than a sharp jerk.
- **Stay aware**
  Practise in a marked, traffic-free route and leave space for others.

---

# A wide, gentle turn

```text
                 LOOK THROUGH THE TURN
                         → → →
       START                         FINISH
         ↓                              ↑
       [bike]  . . . . . . . . . . .  [cone]
                 .                 .
                   .             .
                     .         .
                       .     .
                         . .
                   wide smooth curve
```

- Slow down before the curve if needed.
- Look along the path you want to follow.
- Keep your arms relaxed and steer gradually.
- Leave room to stop if the turn does not feel right.

# Notice hazards and other people

```text
       YOUR ROUTE  → → → → → → →

       . . . . . . . . . . . . . .
                    X  loose gravel?
       . . . . . . . . . . . . . .

    person walking  :)          cone  △
    leave space                go around slowly
```

- Scan the practice route before starting.
- Watch for people, uneven ground, loose gravel, puddles and obstacles.
- Give others space and follow the tutor's instructions.
- These lessons use a traffic-free practice area, not public-road training.

# A simple safe-ride routine

1. **Check**
   Bike, helmet, route and space around you.
2. **Look**
   Look ahead and notice people or hazards.
3. **Slow**
   Reduce speed before a bend or stop point.
4. **Steer**
   Follow a wide, smooth curve with gentle movements.
5. **Stop and reflect**
   Stop in the clear zone and name one thing that went well.

> Public-road cycling needs further instruction and local rules. Practise these skills only in a safe, traffic-free area unless qualified guidance says otherwise.

# Live Demo

> **Ride a wide turn and stop in the clear zone**
> The tutor demonstrates the marked course at a slow, controlled pace.

1. Walk the course together and point out hazards and stopping space.
2. Demonstrate looking through the turn and steering smoothly.
3. Show where to slow down and where to stop.
4. Invite learners to choose a pace that feels safe.

# Workshop Exercises

### Exercise 4.1: Wide turns *8 min*

Practise a gentle turn around widely spaced cones in a traffic-free area. Walk the route first, then ride only if ready. Leave a clear stopping space.

### Exercise 4.2: Skills circuit *10 min*

Combine a gentle start, straight riding, a wide turn and a controlled stop. A partner observes from outside the route and offers one positive note and one practice idea.

### Exercise 4.3: My safe-riding checklist *7 min*

Write or draw a personal checklist: bike check, helmet, clear space, look ahead, slow before turning and stop safely. Add a confidence note to your portfolio.

<!-- _class: takeaway -->

# Keep Going

1. **Practise safely**
   Choose a flat, traffic-free area and a pace that feels comfortable.
2. **Build gradually**
   Repeat starts, straight rides, turns and stops before adding difficulty.
3. **Get further guidance**
   Learn local cycling rules and seek qualified instruction before riding in traffic.

# Your Next Ride

> **Choose one small, safe practice goal**
> Progress comes from steady, comfortable practice—not from going fast.

1. Check your bike and helmet.
2. Ask a trusted helper or qualified instructor if you need support.
3. Stop if tired, unsure or uncomfortable.
