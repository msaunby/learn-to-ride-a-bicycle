# Balance and Bike Basics

## Cycle Skills

Lesson 1

Learn to Ride a Bicycle

---

<!-- _class: cards -->

# Today: feel safe and get started

- **Know your bike**
  Point out the saddle, handlebars, pedals, wheels and brakes.
- **Check before riding**
  Look over the tyres, brakes, chain and fit.
- **Find your balance**
  Start with feet on the ground, then try a gentle walk-and-glide.
- **Build confidence**
  Practise at your own pace in a flat, traffic-free space.

---

# Meet the bicycle

```text
                      handlebars
                          __
                         /  \
        saddle           |  |      front brake
          __             |  |          |
         /  \       _____|  |_____     v
        /____\     /            __\___[ ]
          |       /     frame  /       \
          |      /____________/         \
       __/ \__  O                         O
      (       ) rear wheel            front wheel
       ‾‾‾‾‾‾‾
          pedals + chain
```

- **Hands:** rest on the grips; know where the brakes are.
- **Feet:** wear closed-toe shoes; keep laces clear of the chain.
- **Fit:** you should be able to stand over the bike and reach the controls comfortably.

> **If the bike does not fit or something seems damaged, stop and ask the tutor.**

<!-- _class: steps -->

# A calm pre-ride check

1. **Tyres**
   Look for damage and check they feel firm. Ask for help if unsure.
2. **Brakes**
   Squeeze each lever. The brakes should engage and the bike should stop.
3. **Chain and wheels**
   Look for anything loose, tangled or rubbing.
4. **Saddle and steering**
   Check the saddle is secure and the handlebars turn smoothly.
5. **Helmet and space**
   Put on a correctly fitted helmet. Check the practice area is clear.

> **Do the check before every practice ride.**

---

# Helmet: level and snug

```text
          front edge: about two fingers above eyebrows
                       ↓
                 .-----------.
               .'   HELMET    '.
              /                 \
             |       head        |
              \                 /
               '.___       ___. '
                    \     /
                     \   /  straps meet just below ears
                      \ /
                     [chin]
                 snug, not tight
```

- Keep the helmet level, not tipped back.
- Fasten the buckle and adjust the straps so it stays in place.
- Ask the tutor or a trusted adult to check the fit.

# Workshop: balance at your pace

### Exercise 1.1: Name and check *8 min*

With a partner, point to the bike parts and do a pre-ride check together. Draw and label one part you learned today.

### Exercise 1.2: Mount, stand and step *8 min*

With the bike held still, practise standing over it, holding the grips and putting one foot down safely. Take turns spotting; do not pull or push the rider.

### Exercise 1.3: Walk and glide *9 min*

In a wide, flat, traffic-free area, walk the bike, then try a short, gentle glide with feet ready to touch down. Stop whenever you want.

# Draw the practice space

```text
     START                                      STOP
       |                                          |
       v                                          v
   [bike]  . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .   [cone]
           <------ wide, clear, flat lane ------>

                 Look ahead  → → →
            Feet ready to touch the ground
```

- Keep the lane wide and free of people, traffic and obstacles.
- Practise one short glide at a time.
- A helper walks beside the learner only if the learner wants help.

# Self-Assessment Quiz

1. Which part of the bike helps you steer?
2. Name two things you check before riding.
3. True or false: A helmet should sit level on your head.
4. What can you do if you feel wobbly?
5. What kind of area is suitable for beginner practice?

# Discussion

1. **The bike**
   Which part felt new or unfamiliar?
2. **Balance**
   What helped you feel steady during the walk or glide?
3. **Confidence**
   What would make the next practice feel comfortable?

---

<!-- _class: takeaway -->

# Key Takeaways

1. **Check first**
   Look over the bike, helmet and practice space before riding.
2. **Go gently**
   Start with simple, short practice steps.
3. **Look ahead**
   Keep your eyes up and your feet ready to touch down.
