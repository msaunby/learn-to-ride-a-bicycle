# Today: move and stop with control

- **Pedal steadily**
  Use smooth, comfortable strokes.
- **Know the brakes**
  Find both levers and practise squeezing them while stationary.
- **Brake early**
  Slow down before the stopping point or a turn.
- **Stop safely**
  Come to a controlled stop, then put a foot down.

---

# How pedalling moves the bike

```text
        pedal turns
            ↓
      [CRANK]──chain──────────────┐
                                  v
                           rear wheel turns
                              .------.
                             (   O    )  → forward
                              '------'
```

- Push the pedals in a smooth circle, not in sudden bursts.
- Keep your eyes ahead and your hands relaxed.
- Start with a low, comfortable effort; speed is not the goal.

---

# Brake before the stop point

```text
     LOOK AHEAD                          STOPPING ZONE
         → → →                                ↓
       [bike]  . . . . . . . . . . . .      |  STOP  |
                ease off     squeeze gently  |_______|
                   ↓              ↓
              slow down      come to stop
```

- Learn which lever operates each brake on your bike; this can vary.
- Practise applying the brakes gently and progressively.
- Follow the bike maker's guidance and your tutor's demonstration.
- Once stopped, put a foot down and keep hold of the bike.

> **Avoid grabbing a brake suddenly. Ask the tutor to check the brakes if anything feels wrong.**

# Practise a controlled stop

1. **Choose a short lane**
   Use a flat, wide, traffic-free area with a clear stopping zone.
2. **Ride at walking pace**
   Keep looking ahead and stay relaxed.
3. **Start braking early**
   Ease the brake levers on smoothly as you approach the marker.
4. **Stop and stabilise**
   Once stopped, put a foot down and pause.
5. **Reset**
   Walk the bike back and repeat only when ready.

> The tutor demonstrates the brake setup on the learner's own bicycle before practice.

# Live Demo

> **Pedal steadily, then make a gentle stop**
> Demonstrate at low speed in a clear, traffic-free area.

1. Show the brake levers and identify what each one does on this bike.
2. Pedal smoothly while looking ahead.
3. Apply the brakes progressively before the marked stopping zone.
4. Stop, put a foot down and check that the area is clear before moving again.

# Workshop Exercises

### Exercise 3.1: Smooth pedalling *8 min*

In a wide, clear lane, practise a few steady pedal strokes at a comfortable pace. A partner watches from a safe place and notes one sign of good control.

### Exercise 3.2: Brake and stop *10 min*

With the tutor's guidance, practise a gentle stop before a marker. Repeat at low speed, leaving plenty of stopping room. Save one useful reminder in your portfolio.

### Exercise 3.3: Stop-and-reset *7 min*

Practise riding a short distance, stopping in the clear zone, putting a foot down and pausing. Use your Lesson 2 note if available; otherwise start with a fresh check.

# Draw your stopping plan

```text
   START              SLOW DOWN               STOP
     |                     |                     |
     v                     v                     v
   [bike]  . . . . . . .  [brake]  . . . .   |____|

     Look ahead → → →       gentle pressure

     Clear space after the stop:  . . . . . . .
```

- Put the marker where you can stop without rushing.
- The stopping zone must be clear of other people and obstacles.

<!-- _class: takeaway -->

# Key Takeaways

1. **Pedal smoothly**
   A steady rhythm helps you stay comfortable and in control.
2. **Brake progressively**
   Slow down early and follow your tutor's demo for your bike.
3. **Pause after stopping**
   Stabilise the bike before setting off again.
