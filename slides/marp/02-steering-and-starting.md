# Today: move off and steer

- **Recall balance**
  Begin with a bike and space check.
- **Start smoothly**
  Push off gently; keep a foot ready until you feel steady.
- **Steer softly**
  Use small handlebar movements and look where you want to go.
- **Ride straight**
  Practise a short, wide lane in a traffic-free area.

---

# Look where you want to go

```text
                   EYES
                    ↓
     START       LOOK AHEAD                      FINISH
       |              → → →                        |
       v                                           v
     ( O )   . . . . . . . . . . . . . . . .   [cone]
      /|\          wide straight lane
      / \

   small steering adjustments  ↶  ↷
```

- Your bike tends to travel towards where you look and steer.
- Relax your shoulders; hold the grips without squeezing hard.
- Make small changes. Sudden turns can make the bike wobble.

<!-- _class: compare -->

# Smooth control vs. sudden movement

### Smooth control

- Look ahead
- Keep the bike upright
- Make gentle steering movements
- Practise in a wide, clear area

### Sudden movement

- Stare at the front wheel
- Grip the bars tightly
- Jerk the handlebars
- Rush into a narrow course

# A simple start sequence

1. **Set up**
   Choose a clear, flat starting place; check the bike and helmet.
2. **Get ready**
   Hold both grips and place one foot on the pedal in a comfortable position.
3. **Push off gently**
   Use the other foot to push the ground, then bring it to the pedal when ready.
4. **Look ahead**
   Keep the bike upright and steer with small movements.
5. **Slow and stop**
   Use the brakes gently, then put a foot down when stopped.

> Practise only at a pace that feels safe. A helper can walk nearby, but should not steer or hold the moving bike unless agreed and trained to do so.

# Workshop Exercises

### Exercise 2.1: Set-up and push-off *8 min*

Take turns preparing the bike and practising a gentle push-off in a wide lane. The partner observes from the side and offers one encouraging note.

### Exercise 2.2: Follow the line *10 min*

Ride or walk the bike along a straight line made with chalk or cones. Look towards the end of the lane and make small corrections. Save a drawing of your route.

### Exercise 2.3: Start, glide, stop *7 min*

Repeat the start and glide, then stop in the marked clear zone. Record what helped you feel steady; bring the note to Lesson 3.

# Draw the lane

```text
       start                               clear stopping zone
         ↓                                           ↓
       [A]   .    .    .    .    .    .    .      [STOP]
             |<------- wide practice lane ------->|

      Rider looks to the end  → → →
      Partner watches from a safe place beside the lane
```

- Leave enough room to wobble or stop without reaching another rider.
- Never practise toward a road, slope, wall or parked vehicle.

<!-- _class: takeaway -->

# Key Takeaways

1. **Prepare**
   Check your bike and give yourself a wide, clear route.
2. **Look ahead**
   Your gaze helps guide your direction.
3. **Steer gently**
   Small movements and a calm pace help you stay in control.
