# Ways of Working on This Course

This guide explains how the course runs, what to bring to each lesson, what you will create, and what is worth keeping afterwards. Read it before lesson 1, and come back to it before each session.

## How the lessons work

- There are **[N] lessons of about an hour each**. A session may cover one lesson, or two or three with a short break.
- Every lesson follows the same pattern: a recap of the previous lesson, a short mini-lecture with a live demo, a workshop, a self-assessment quiz, then discussion and a wrap-up that tells you what to bring next time.
- Workshops are done **in pairs or small groups**. Expect to swap roles and to give your partner honest feedback.
- **Working alone?** Every lesson has *Self-study notes* that adapt the exercises for one person. Quiz answers are hidden in collapsible sections so you can check your own work. Instead of the group discussion, answer one discussion question in writing.
